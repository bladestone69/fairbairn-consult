## Personal advice, supported by experience

I believe that good financial advice starts with listening. Every person has a different story, and the right plan should reflect your circumstances, priorities and goals.

I completed my BCom degree in 2002 and began my financial advice career with Old Mutual in 2005. Today, I work with Fairbairn Consult, an Old Mutual-owned broker services company, as a representative under Fairbairn Consult (Pty) Ltd, FSP 9328.

## A personal approach, with useful technology

Computers and technology have always been a particular interest of mine. I use modern computing tools, including AI, to support my advice process and help make complex information clearer. Technology supports the work; your circumstances, our conversations and professional judgment remain central to the advice process.

My aim is to provide clear explanations, thoughtful recommendations and continuity over time. Where ongoing service applies, we review your arrangements as your needs and circumstances change.

## How we work together

- We begin by understanding your position, questions and objectives.
- We gather and assess the relevant information.
- I explain suitable recommendations and their supporting information.
- I assist with agreed implementation and follow-up.
- We review your arrangements over time where ongoing service applies.
